About

IT support, development, and community access in Douglas County, Oregon.

About

freesoftware.support is run by David Hamner, with thirteen years of IT experience ranging from programming to managed service providers. David helped bring the Librem 5 to market and has built a career around open-source software and development. Full background on LinkedIn.

We also have a YouTube channel where you can see the kind of work we do. The channel has been through a big shift recently, moving from Linux phone content toward community IT support.

How We Operate

freesoftware.support is a small IT shop offering business-rate support, custom development, and a Community Access tier for people who can't afford paid help. The name "free software" has a double meaning: free as in cost for those who need it, and free as in freedom because open-source tools are at the core of everything we build.

We work with businesses at standard rates and keep a Community Access tier open for everyone else, funded in part by Patreon supporters. Whether you're paying $75/hr or nothing, you get the same tools and the same effort.

Tools and Approach

We leverage AI extensively to deliver efficient support. Email support is handled by an AI-powered system. Phone calls use local AI models for transcription and notes. We use Claude for complex troubleshooting. The goal is to solve your problem using whatever works.

The tools behind this are open source wherever possible:

  • RustDesk for remote support
  • Callen handles phone management, ticketing, and AI-powered email
  • This website is a static site with no tracking or analytics

About Recordings

All calls are recorded for ticket tracking and internal quality purposes. This applies to every tier.

Business and Development clients: Sessions are never published. NDA available.

Community Access clients: Sessions are only published on YouTube with explicit consent. You can opt out at any time. Your support is the same either way.
