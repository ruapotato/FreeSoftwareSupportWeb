About

Who's behind this, and why are we doing it?

Hi, I'm David

I run a YouTube channel where you can see the kind of content I create and get a sense of who I am. If you're thinking about calling, that's a good place to start.

My channel has been a big shift recently. I used to focus primarily on Linux phones, but I've moved toward supporting my local community wherever I can with tech. I'm just one guy trying to make a difference, hoping that predatory business practices aren't required for giving and getting good tech support.

Why This Exists

I think it's wrong to lock people into contracts and squeeze them for as much money as possible. That's how most of the tech support industry works, and it turns what should be a simple thing into a huge burden. Instead of predatory sales tactics, I'm just going to do my best to fix your computer. Good karma out, good karma in. Very simple.

Everyone deserves access to tech support, whether you're a retiree trying to set up email or a student whose laptop is acting up before a deadline. The service is pay what you can. If you can give back, great. If you can't, you still get the same help.

The Philosophy

The name "free software" has a double meaning, and both are intentional:

  • Free as in cost - you will never be charged for help
  • Free as in freedom - I believe in open-source software and will recommend it where it makes sense

That said, I'm not here to lecture anyone. If you're running Windows and it works for you, great. I'll help you with that too. If you're curious about Linux or open-source alternatives, I'm happy to show you what's out there. No pressure either way.

The Recording Thing

Let's talk about the elephant in the room. Yes, calls are recorded and may end up on YouTube.

Here's why: this is community service, and recording is how it stays sustainable. Each call gets turned into a video that helps other people with the same problem, and the ad revenue helps recoup the time spent helping people for free. Without the recordings, there's no way to keep this going long-term.

I'm upfront about this because I think honesty is more important than making the pitch sound perfect. You'll always know you're being recorded. If that's not for you, no hard feelings.

AI-Assisted, Human-Directed

This is a one-person operation, but AI makes it feasible. When you email us, an AI-powered support system reads your message and responds to help troubleshoot. When I'm on a call, I use AI tools to research problems and find solutions faster. The goal is to get your problem solved using whatever works best.

Open Source All the Way Down

The tools behind this project are open source wherever possible, and so is the project itself:

  • RustDesk for remote support, open source and privacy-respecting
  • Callen is the system that handles phone management, ticketing, and AI-powered email replies
  • This website is a simple static site with no tracking, no cookies, no creepy analytics
  • I recommend free and open-source software to callers whenever there's a good option

Best-Effort Support

I want to be upfront: this is a best-effort service. I use every tool at my disposal including AI to work through problems. But some things are beyond what anyone can fix over a phone or email, and I can't promise a solution every time.

While the risk of anything going wrong during a remote session is very small, I also can't guarantee the integrity of your system or data. By using this service, you accept that freesoftware.support is not responsible for any damages, data loss, or system issues that may occur. This is free help offered in good faith, not a contract.
