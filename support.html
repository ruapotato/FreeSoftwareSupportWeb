How the Money Works

freesoftware.support is a one-person operation. There's no company behind it, no investors, no grants. The service stays running because of people who give back.

Monthly Support

The single best way to support this project. A $5/month subscription is more valuable than a large one-time payment because it keeps things predictable and sustainable.

Where the Money Goes

In the interest of full transparency:

  • AI costs - Anthropic/Claude API usage for email support and troubleshooting
  • Power and hardware - local AI models handle text-to-speech, speech-to-text, and local language models, all of which are heavy power consumers
  • Phone line - a dedicated number for support calls
  • David's time - researching problems, taking calls, editing videos
  • Software and equipment - recording, editing, and publishing tools

That's it. No overhead, no office, no middle management. Every contribution goes directly to keeping this running. If you're curious about exactly how all of this works, the entire system is open source in the Callen repo.
