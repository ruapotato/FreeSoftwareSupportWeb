What Community Access Is

freesoftware.support offers paid IT services at business rates. But we also maintain a Community Access tier for people who can't afford paid support. Same tools, same effort, pay what you can including nothing. Patreon and PayPal donations are what keep this tier funded.

Where It Goes

  • AI costs - Anthropic/Claude API for email support and troubleshooting
  • Power and hardware - local AI models for speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and local language models
  • Phone line - dedicated support number
  • Time - researching problems, taking calls, editing videos

The entire system is open source in the Callen repo.
