Fill this out and we'll get your site set up. You can also email all of this to hello@freesoftware.support if you prefer.

Your website will live at yourname.freesoft.page. A subdomain is just the part before the dot. Pick something short and easy to remember, like your business name. For example:

  • Tim's Tools → tims-tools.freesoft.page
  • Roseburg Pet Grooming → roseburg-pets.freesoft.page
  • Jane's Bakery → janes-bakery.freesoft.page

Lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens only.

Want to add photos or a logo? Once we get your base website created, you can email images to hello@freesoftware.support and we'll add them for you.
